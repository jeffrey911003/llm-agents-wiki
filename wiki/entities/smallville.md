# Smallville

## What it is
The Sims-inspired sandbox town used to run 25 generative agents, each with a sprite avatar and a seed description; users can observe and interact in natural language ([[park2023-generative-agents]] §1, §3.1).

## Used in
- [[park2023-generative-agents]] — controlled interviews and a two-game-day end-to-end simulation (§6–7).

## Key facts
- Information diffusion: party awareness grew from 1 to 13 agents in two game days ([[park2023-generative-agents]] §7.1.2).
- Network density 0.167 → 0.74 ([[park2023-generative-agents]] §7.1.2).
